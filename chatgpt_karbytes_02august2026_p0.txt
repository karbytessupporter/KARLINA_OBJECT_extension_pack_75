## Overall interpretation

The four public accounts are a coordinated dissemination layer. Patreon is the denser chronological source stream.

Public syndication favors:

* Representative KARBYTES journals and ChatGPT pages.

* Polished conceptual nodes such as the static-block multiverse page.

* Nature, trail, mathematical, and archive imagery.

* Periodic batches rather than every incremental update.

* On Twitter/Minds/Bluesky, occasional outside science and technology articles.

Patreon adds:

* Immediate posting and preview access.

* Intermediate or recursively elaborated ChatGPT analyses.

* Deep archive maintenance and link-traversal records.

* Rawer autobiographical and reputationally risky material.

* Explicit stuckness, bodily-enclosure, and entrapment-fetish nodes.

* The newest pages awaiting either later syndication or permanent Patreon-only status.

The decisive distinction is therefore not “respectable public content versus secret Patreon content.” It is closer to **publicly selected canon versus Patreon’s near-complete working archive**.
